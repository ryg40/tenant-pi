# Glossary

The words that the Walkr guides use. Each entry names a word to avoid in its place.
Walkr has two modes: Compare and Feedback. The words of the retired modes (Core, Double, Coinflip, Trainer) are in the glossary of the release tag `portable/20261122-548259a`.

## Compare

[Compare](docs/compare.md) shows 2 or 3 prepared options of one change for one pick.

**Option**:
One complete prepared alternative of a Compare session: a rendered HTML document or a text.
_Avoid_: Candidate, version

**Pick**:
The one option that the user selected and submitted, or none. The first submitted pick is final.
_Avoid_: Winner, keeper, vote

**Workflow**:
The type of a Compare session, for example `cfn-options` or `text-revision`. It names the display format and the steps of the agent.
_Avoid_: Mode

**Rating**:
The judgment of the user on one session: a whole number from 1 to 10, shown as five stars with half stars.
_Avoid_: Score, rank

**Outcome**:
The short report of the agent on what it did with the pick.
_Avoid_: Result

**Session**:
One saved Compare record: the brief, the options, the result, the ratings and the outcome.
_Avoid_: Run, review

**Session list**:
The list of the saved Compare sessions in the page, the newest first.
_Avoid_: Dashboard, home page

**Mirror**:
A persistent, read-only Compare page over a mirror root that holds copies of the session records of one or more computers ([mirror](mirror/README.md)).
_Avoid_: History mirror
